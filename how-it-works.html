How Tutorly Works

We handle the vetting and logistics so you can focus on your child's success.

Trust & Safety

Our 4-Step Vetting Process

Less than 10% of applicants make it onto the Tutorly platform. When you see a "Verified" badge, it means the tutor has passed our rigorous safety and quality checks.

1

Identity & Background Check

Comprehensive national and county criminal background checks, plus identity verification via government-issued ID.

2

Credential Verification

We manually verify university degrees, teaching licenses, and any specialized certifications claimed on their profile.

3

Subject Matter Testing

Tutors must pass rigorous proficiency exams in the specific subjects they wish to teach.

4

Live Interview

A face-to-face interview to assess communication skills, teaching philosophy, and overall professionalism.

The Parent Experience

Everything you need to manage your child's education, all in one place.

1. Find the Perfect Match

Use our advanced filters to find a verified tutor who matches your child's specific needs, learning style, and your family's schedule.

2. Seamless Booking

No more texting back and forth. Book, reschedule, or cancel sessions directly through the intuitive calendar in your Parent Dashboard.

3. Track Progress

After every session, you'll receive a detailed Progress Note outlining what was covered, areas of improvement, and homework assignments.
