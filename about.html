About Tutorly

Empowering students through verified, trusted, and personalized education.

Our Mission

Building Trust in Private Education

Tutorly was founded by parents, for parents. We recognized how difficult and stressful it was to find a qualified, reliable, and trustworthy tutor for our own children. The process was opaque, and safety was always a lingering concern.

We built this platform with a single goal: to make discovering and booking exceptional educators completely stress-free. By enforcing the strictest vetting process in the industry, we guarantee that every tutor on our platform is someone you'll feel safe inviting into your home.

Our Uncompromising Vetting Process

Identity & Background

Every applicant undergoes a rigorous SSN trace, national and county criminal background check, and sex offender registry screening. We don't cut corners when it comes to your family's safety.

Academic Verification

Tutors must provide proof of their educational background. We manually verify university degrees, state teaching licenses, and any specialized certificates listed on their profiles.

Live Interviews

Beyond algorithms and tests, our internal team conducts a live video interview with every candidate to assess communication skills, patience, empathy, and teaching style.
