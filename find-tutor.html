How to Choose the Right Tutor

Every profile above is already vetted. These three checks help you narrow the shortlist down to the one educator who fits your child best.

Match the Goal

Filter by subject and grade level first, then read the profile summary. A tutor who specialises in exam preparation works differently from one who rebuilds core confidence over a full term.

Check the Schedule

Consistency matters more than intensity. Confirm the tutor has a recurring weekly slot that suits your household before booking, so sessions become a routine rather than a scramble.

Start With a Trial

Book a single introductory session before committing to a bundle. If the teaching style isn't the right fit, switch tutors at no extra cost and keep your remaining hours.
